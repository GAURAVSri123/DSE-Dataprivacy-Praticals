# Practical 3: Regulation Compliance Assessment of Coursera

## Aim

To examine the privacy regulations applicable to **Coursera** and assess how its privacy practices address important legal and regulatory requirements related to the protection and processing of personal data.

---

## Organization

**Coursera** is an online learning platform that provides courses, professional certificates, degree programs, assessments, and other educational services. To provide these services, Coursera collects and processes different categories of personal information, including account, course, device, identity-verification, and usage information.

---

## Applicable Privacy Regulations

The major privacy frameworks and regulations relevant to Coursera include:

### 1. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

The GDPR applies where Coursera processes personal data of individuals covered by the regulation. Coursera's privacy documentation describes GDPR-related legal bases, individual rights, and international data-transfer mechanisms.

### 2. U.S. State Privacy Laws / CCPA

Coursera provides a dedicated privacy notice addressing California privacy rights and other applicable U.S. state privacy requirements. It describes categories of personal information, purposes of processing, recipients, and certain opt-out rights.

### 3. Data Privacy Framework (DPF)

Coursera states that it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the UK extension, and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certain international transfers of personal data.

### 4. Indian Data Protection Requirements

Coursera operates internationally, including through Coursera India Private Limited. Where Indian data-protection requirements apply to its processing activities, those requirements must also be considered in assessing its processing of personal data in India. Coursera's enterprise data-protection documentation identifies Coursera India Private Limited as an engineering and customer-support entity.

---

## Personal Data Processed

According to Coursera's privacy notice, the platform may process information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Date of birth
* Address
* Username and password
* Course and assessment information
* Assignment and quiz information
* Education, career, and skills information
* IP address
* Device information
* Usage information
* Identity-verification information
* Biometric facial data in certain identity-verification processes

## Major Privacy Concerns

### 1. Biometric Data

Identity verification can involve facial biometric information. Because biometric information can be highly sensitive, strong security controls and limited retention are important.

### 2. Third-Party Processing

Coursera uses contractors and service providers to support its operations. This creates a need for appropriate contractual, security, and monitoring controls. Coursera's enterprise DPA lists multiple subprocessors and their processing locations.

### 3. International Data Transfers

Coursera operates internationally and may process data across different countries. Its documentation describes transfer mechanisms including Data Privacy Frameworks and Standard Contractual Clauses.

### 4. Extensive Usage Data

An online learning platform can process information about course participation, assessments, device activity, and interactions with the platform. Such information should be collected and used only for appropriate and disclosed purposes.

### 5. Advertising and Tracking

Coursera's U.S. privacy documentation describes certain disclosures and targeted/personalized advertising and provides opt-out mechanisms.

---

## Recommended Compliance Measures

The following measures can strengthen privacy protection:

1. Apply **data minimization** to collect only necessary information.
2. Encrypt personal and sensitive information during storage and transmission.
3. Apply strict access controls to biometric and identity-verification information.
4. Use **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** for administrative accounts.
5. Maintain clear retention and secure-deletion schedules.
6. Regularly audit third-party service providers and subprocessors.
7. Use appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.
8. Provide users with simple mechanisms to exercise privacy rights.
9. Conduct regular privacy and security assessments.
10. Clearly inform users when AI, analytics, cookies, or automated processing are used.
11. Maintain audit logs for access to sensitive information.
12. Provide a process for correcting inaccurate personal information.

## Result

The regulation compliance assessment of **Coursera** was successfully performed. The assessment identified the major privacy regulations and frameworks relevant to its operations and examined its practices concerning data collection, lawful processing, user rights, security, retention, biometric information, third-party processing, and international data transfers.

---

## Conclusion

Coursera processes a wide range of personal information to provide online educational services. Its published privacy documentation describes mechanisms related to GDPR, U.S. state privacy laws, international data transfers, user rights, security, retention, and identity verification.

The assessment shows that privacy compliance is not limited to having a privacy policy. Organizations must also consider **data minimization, security, third-party processing, biometric information, international transfers, retention, and users' privacy rights** throughout the data lifecycle.

Therefore, continuous privacy assessment and periodic review of data-processing practices are important for maintaining effective privacy protection as the platform and applicable regulations evolve.
